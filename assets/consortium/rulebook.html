Setup

Enable Consortium in the lobby. The Consortium map is selected and locked automatically — terrain, frontier unlock and the Consortium milestones require it. The expansion adds a shared iridium bank, two new card tags, three new terrain types, cooperative megastructures (including three Bridges), a Core Sampling standard project, and its own milestones and awards.

Recommended player count is 3–4. Play otherwise follows the base rules unless this rulebook says otherwise.

Iridium

Iridium is a new player resource — a metal stockpile like steel or titanium, shown on your player board after heat. It is not a production track.

Where it comes from

  • Crater fields: when you place a tile on a crater-field space, you gain 1 iridium the first time that space is claimed (once per space for the game).
  • Core Sampling: a Consortium standard project — pay 6 M€ to take 1 iridium from the bank (see below).
  • Some Consortium cards and megastructure effects also grant iridium.

The bank and the 28 cap

All iridium in the game comes from one shared bank that starts with 28 iridium. When you gain iridium, it leaves the bank. When you spend it, it returns to the bank. Nothing creates new iridium beyond that pool.

If the bank is empty, crater fields still mark their bonus as claimed but grant nothing, and Core Sampling cannot be used until someone spends iridium back into the bank.

What it can pay for

Iridium is worth 4 M€ per unit when paying, but only for:

  • Cards with the Structure tag
  • Cards with the Prospecting tag
  • Megastructure segments (including keystones)

You cannot spend iridium as general-purpose money on ordinary cards the way titanium works for space cards. Held iridium is worth 0 VP at game end — it is currency, not score.

Why there is no production

Iridium has no production row. Supply is deliberately scarce and shared. The only card that breaks this pattern is Siderophile Extraction, a special exception — treat it as unique, not as the start of an iridium economy.

New tags

TagMeaning
Structure Engineering and construction cards tied to megastructures and durable works. Iridium may be spent when playing these cards.
Prospecting Surveying and mineral cards. Iridium may be spent when playing these cards.

Both tags count for ordinary tag effects (Diversifier-style checks, tag-based cards, and so on) just like Building or Science.

Megastructures

Each game has five megastructures on a shared track panel:

  • Three Bridges — always present, one for each frontier sector
  • Two grand structures — drawn at random from Space Elevator, L1 Magnetic Shield, Mohole, Solar Mirror, and Arcology

Contributing segments

On your turn, as a standard action, you may contribute one segment to one megastructure. Pay the listed cost (M€, and you may use steel, titanium, and iridium toward that payment). Your cube marks ownership of that segment.

  • Bridges have 4 segments (first three cost 12 M€; keystone 8 M€).
  • Grand structures have 6 segments (first five cost 16 M€; keystone 12 M€).

The keystone

The last segment on every structure is the keystone. In addition to its M€ cost, the keystone always requires a minimum iridium spend (2 on a Bridge, 3 on a grand structure). You cannot finish a structure without iridium.

Foundation gates

Before you place your first segment on Space Elevator, Mohole, or Solar Mirror, you must already own a tile on a highland space. Bridges, L1 Magnetic Shield, and Arcology have no foundation requirement.

What completion grants

When the keystone is placed, the structure completes immediately:

  1. Each contributor scores 1 VP per segment they paid for.
  2. The keystone player scores an extra 2 VP.
  3. A global effect applies to everyone, and each contributor gets a stronger personal bonus (see the track tooltips in play).
Half-finished structures score nothing. Segments on an incomplete megastructure at game end are sunk cost — 0 VP. Finish them or write them off.

Bridges and the frontier

The Consortium map has a frontier beyond the main disc — locked spaces that begin the game unplaceable. Completing a Bridge opens exactly one sector:

  • Frontier spaces tied to that Bridge become placeable.
  • Chasms in that sector become ordinary land.
  • Other sectors stay locked until their Bridge finishes.

Racing a Bridge opens new land and ocean opportunities. Ignoring Bridges keeps the map cramped. Incomplete Bridges still score nothing if unfinished.

Core Sampling

Consortium adds a standard project: Core Sampling.

  • Cost: 6 M€
  • Effect: gain 1 iridium from the bank
  • Unavailable when the bank is empty

Use it when you need iridium for a Structure/Prospecting play or a keystone and the board has no free crater fields left.

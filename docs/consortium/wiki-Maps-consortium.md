## Consortium[](consortium)

Fan expansion maps ([terraforming-mars-consortium](https://github.com/MikkelFrid/terraforming-mars-consortium)). Same hex geometry; terrain overlays differ. Full rules: https://terraforming-mars-consortium-production.up.railway.app/assets/consortium/rulebook.html#board

### Massif
![massif](https://terraforming-mars-consortium-production.up.railway.app/assets/consortium/maps/massif.png)

Balanced default — even craters, mid-width chasms, open early game.

### Rift Basin
![rift-basin](https://terraforming-mars-consortium-production.up.railway.app/assets/consortium/maps/rift.png)

Iridium hunger — wide chasms, scarce core craters, rich locked frontier.

### Archipelago
![archipelago](https://terraforming-mars-consortium-production.up.railway.app/assets/consortium/maps/archipelago.png)

Structure play — more highlands, narrower chasms, wider open frontier.
